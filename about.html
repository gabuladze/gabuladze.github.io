I'm a software engineer in Tbilisi. Nine years in, since July 2017. Most of that time has gone into backend systems that move money.

JavaScript and TypeScript have been the constant. Go and Solidity arrived in 2022, when I joined Chiliz to work on a public blockchain and the services around it. Blockchain is the domain I work in, not the job description. The problems are the ones you get anywhere money moves. Failures have to be recoverable. Someone has to be able to see what the system did, and why.

Before Chiliz I spent four and a half years at Biznetx, building a crypto payment platform and the tools the operations team used to run it.

Outside work I build iPhone apps. Ripasso is the one I still use.
